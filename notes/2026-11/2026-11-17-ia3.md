# (IA)³: Infused Adapter by Inhibiting and Amplifying Inner Activations

**Paper:** Liu et al., 2022, "Few-Shot Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning is Better and Cheaper than In-Context Learning" ([arXiv:2205.05638](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05638))

## Idea
Instead of adding low-rank matrices like LoRA, (IA)³ learns three vectors that rescale keys, values, and the intermediate activations of the feed-forward block. That is one elementwise multiply per targeted module, so the number of trainable parameters is about 0.01% of the model.

## Why it matters
- Far fewer trainable parameters than LoRA, and nothing to merge except a per-channel scale.
- Mixed-task batches are cheap, because each example only needs its own scaling vectors.

## Implementation detail worth remembering
In PEFT, the feed-forward modules are scaled on their **input** and the attention modules on their **output**, so the learned vector's shape depends on which group a module is in. This means the `feedforward_modules` matching has to agree exactly with `target_modules`. If they disagree, a saved adapter can fail to load with a shape mismatch.

## What I'd try
Compare (IA)³ and LoRA r=8 on a small instruction-tuning set at equal wall-clock time, and check how sensitive (IA)³ is to the learning rate.
